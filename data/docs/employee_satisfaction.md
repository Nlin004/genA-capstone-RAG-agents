# Employee Satisfaction Survey Summary

## Purpose

The company runs an anonymous employee satisfaction survey every quarter
to track engagement and catch problems early. This document summarizes
the most recent results and how they are used.

## Survey Methodology

The survey is sent to all employees and covers five dimensions: manager
relationship, career growth, workload sustainability, compensation
fairness, and overall engagement. Each dimension is scored on a 1-5 scale,
and participation is voluntary but strongly encouraged; typical
participation is above 80%.

## Recent Results

The most recent survey showed an overall engagement score of 3.9 out of
5, up slightly from 3.7 the prior quarter. Career growth remains the
lowest-scoring dimension at 3.4, while manager relationship scored
highest at 4.2. Workload sustainability scored 3.6, with written comments
most frequently mentioning tight deadlines during quarter-end pushes.

## How Results Are Used

Scores are broken down by department and shared with each department
lead, along with anonymized written comments. Department leads are
expected to discuss the results with their team and propose at least one
concrete action item per quarter tied to their lowest-scoring dimension.
HR tracks these action items and reports progress company-wide at the
following quarterly all-hands.

## Follow-Up Actions From This Cycle

Based on the career-growth score, HR is piloting a formal internal
mobility program and expanding the manager training curriculum to include
more coaching on growth conversations. Based on workload feedback, two
teams are trialing a quieter "focus week" around quarter-end with no new
feature commitments.

## Confidentiality

Individual responses are never shared with managers; only aggregated,
anonymized results at the team level (minimum team size of five) are
reported, to protect respondents from being identified.
