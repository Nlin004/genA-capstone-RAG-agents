# Customer Complaint Handling

## Purpose

This document describes how the company receives, triages, and resolves
customer complaints so that every customer gets a timely, consistent
response.

## Intake Channels

Complaints arrive through three channels: the support email inbox, the
in-app chat widget, and escalations forwarded from the sales team. All
three feed into the same ticketing system so no complaint is handled
outside of a tracked ticket.

## Triage and Severity Levels

Every incoming complaint is triaged within four business hours into one
of three severity levels. Severity 1 covers a full service outage or a
security/privacy concern and must be acknowledged within 30 minutes.
Severity 2 covers a significant feature not working for one customer and
must be acknowledged within four hours. Severity 3 covers minor issues,
cosmetic bugs, or feature requests, acknowledged within one business day.

## Resolution Process

The assigned support agent owns the ticket end-to-end: reproducing the
issue, looping in engineering if it is a confirmed bug, and communicating
status to the customer at least once every 24 hours until resolved.
Customers are never left without an update for more than one business
day, even if the update is simply "still investigating."

## Escalation Path

If a customer expresses significant dissatisfaction, threatens to churn,
or a complaint remains open past its severity-level SLA, the ticket is
escalated to the Customer Success manager for that account, who may loop
in a director if needed.

## Closing the Loop

After resolution, the agent asks the customer to confirm the issue is
fixed before closing the ticket. Repeated complaints from the same
customer, or any Severity 1 incident, trigger a post-mortem reviewed by
the Customer Success and Engineering leads together, with any resulting
process or product changes tracked to completion.
